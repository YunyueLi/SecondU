Derived from DeepSeek Harness office-pptx. Inspect the supplied deck's theme, slide size, layouts and repeated elements before editing. Establish the audience, decision or message, narrative sequence and slide count from the actual task. Each slide should communicate a clear claim with evidence; do not invent metrics, citations, customers or images.

Use `pptxgenjs` for authoring. The helper's pptx command supports a title and slides containing a title and bullet list; use a task-local script for designed layouts, tables, charts, images and speaker notes. Preserve editable elements where possible. A newly rebuilt deck is not a faithful edit of an existing deck with media or complex layouts; inspect and explicitly preserve those features.

Check the saved PPTX for slide count, text, charts, notes and relationships. Render every newly designed slide or each edited slide, then visually inspect clipping, overlap, alignment, type hierarchy, contrast and chart labels. Verify that the font choices exist or state substitutions. Rendering does not prove animation, embedded media or live data refresh works.
