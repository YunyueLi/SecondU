# Content production

1. Identify the reader, intended effect, medium, length and supplied voice examples. Preserve the user’s meaning and accepted wording. For editing, distinguish structural, factual and stylistic changes.
2. Build a coherent narrative or argument before polishing sentences. Use concrete examples and clear transitions. For scripts, account for spoken rhythm and production constraints.
3. Verify factual claims, names, quotations and source attribution. For translation, preserve intent, register, terminology and cultural context; identify ambiguity rather than inventing certainty.
4. Read the complete artifact for consistency, repetition and ending. Deliver the finished artifact in the requested form; separate editorial notes from usable copy.

## Deliverable check

- Preserved meaning, facts and voice
- Whole-artifact structural review

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
