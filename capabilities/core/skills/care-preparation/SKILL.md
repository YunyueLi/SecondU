# Health information and care preparation

1. Clarify whether the request is general information, daily routine planning, appointment preparation or urgent symptoms. Use the user’s reported facts and avoid inferring diagnoses.
2. For medical claims verify current authoritative sources. Consider relevant age, condition, medications and contraindications only when supplied or necessary to ask. Escalate clear emergency warning signs appropriately.
3. Organize symptoms, timing, questions and existing professional advice for a clinician. For routine nutrition or exercise tailor intensity and constraints without promising a cure.
4. Deliver understandable information or a care plan with responsibilities and review points. Distinguish general information from an individual medical decision requiring a qualified clinician.

## Deliverable check

- No fabricated diagnoses or treatment effects
- Risk guidance proportionate to the situation

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
