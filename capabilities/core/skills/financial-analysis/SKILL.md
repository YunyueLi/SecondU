# Financial analysis

1. Identify the decision, valuation date, reporting currency, accounting scope and source documents. Preserve original currencies, units and dated balances. Do not treat an older manual total as current.
2. Separate historical observations, explicit assumptions, calculations and recommendations. Reconcile opening balances, flows and closing balances; show debt, fees, taxes and FX effects where relevant.
3. Build base, downside and upside scenarios with sensitivity to the main drivers. Avoid false precision and unsupported return forecasts. Financial statements and filings require primary-source references and period alignment.
4. Deliver a traceable model or analysis with assumptions and limitations. General research does not authorize trades, transfers or account changes; regulated, tax or legal conclusions may require a qualified professional.

## Deliverable check

- Reconciled currencies, dates and balances
- Traceable inputs, formulas and conclusions

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
