# Operations and career

1. Determine the desired outcome, stakeholders, constraints and existing process. For career work use supplied experience and original records; never invent achievements, dates or interview quotes.
2. Map responsibilities, handoffs, deadlines, dependencies and escalation. Turn meeting statements into decisions, open questions and actions with owners; keep tentative ideas distinct.
3. For resumes and interviews connect claims to examples, personal contribution, constraints and outcomes. For operations identify bottlenecks and capacity before proposing schedules.
4. Deliver a usable plan, document or rehearsal. Check ownership, timing and internal consistency; mark unanswered questions without turning them into fabricated commitments.

## Deliverable check

- No invented experience or outcomes
- Clear dependencies and open decisions

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
