# Commercial planning

1. Establish the counterpart, buying process, decision criteria, actual pain and the user’s objective. Separate confirmed facts from inferred motives.
2. Translate the offer into scoped deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, responsibilities, timeline and commercial terms. Compare alternatives and dependencies.
3. For negotiations identify priorities, acceptable ranges, alternatives, concessions and questions. For customer success define adoption milestones, ownership and escalation triggers.
4. Produce the requested proposal, meeting brief or draft message. Check price arithmetic and consistency between scope and promises. Sending or accepting terms requires the user’s authorization.

## Deliverable check

- Promises match deliverable scope
- Auditable pricing and material terms

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
