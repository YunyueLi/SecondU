# Travel research and planning

1. Identify dates, origin, travelers, budget, interests, mobility needs and confirmed bookings. Preserve timezones and distinguish departure from arrival dates.
2. Verify schedules, official entry requirements, opening hours and booking conditions that affect feasibility. Prices and availability are time-stamped observations, not guarantees.
3. Build routes with transfer, meal, rest and disruption buffers. Compare total costs and cancellation terms; mark assumptions and optional activities.
4. Deliver a workable itinerary with source links and alternatives. Booking, payment and final submission require the user’s specific authorization.

## Deliverable check

- Feasible timezones, transfers and opening hours
- Dated price and rule checks

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
