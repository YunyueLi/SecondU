# Legal and privacy research

1. Identify jurisdiction, effective date, parties, document version and the practical decision. For a contract read definitions, cross-references and surrounding clauses before evaluating a sentence.
2. Separate the text’s explicit obligations, interpretation, business risk and open questions. Cite exact clause locations or authoritative rules; verify current law when the task depends on it.
3. For privacy map actual data, purpose, recipients, retention, access and deletion behavior. Compare proposed controls to actual implementation, not policy wording alone.
4. Deliver a clause comparison, issue list or review draft with alternatives. State when a conclusion depends on missing facts or qualified legal advice; do not imply professional representation.

## Deliverable check

- Clear jurisdiction, date and version
- Traceable clauses or primary authority

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
