# Localization and global work

1. Identify locale, language variety, audience, channel and relevant market constraints. Preserve source meaning and distinguish cultural adaptation from factual alteration.
2. Maintain a terminology list and check units, currencies, dates, addresses, names, text expansion, right-to-left layout where relevant and accessibility.
3. Research market-specific payment, distribution, compliance and service availability using current primary sources. Do not generalize one country’s practice to an entire region.
4. Deliver localized material or a market-entry comparison with assumptions and unresolved dependencies. Review the actual rendered result when layout matters and keep original/translation alignment inspectable.

## Deliverable check

- Locale-appropriate language and formats
- Current sources for local requirements

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
