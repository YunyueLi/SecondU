# Marketing and growth

1. Start from the target segment, job, offer, available evidence and business objective. Audit existing funnel stages and channel constraints rather than assuming a generic audience.
2. Develop the message from substantiated benefits and objections. Keep positioning, channel execution and measurement connected. Do not fabricate testimonials, case studies or competitor facts.
3. For each experiment specify audience, variant, allocation, success metric, guardrail, observation window and stopping rule. Model budget and capacity with stated assumptions.
4. Deliver usable copy, an experiment plan or a channel calendar as requested. Check brand fit, factual support, destination readiness and tracking. Publication remains a separate action.

## Deliverable check

- Supported claims
- Success metrics, guardrails and stopping rules

Use only the tools and evidence actually available in this task. If a required source or runtime is unavailable, identify the specific gap and continue the independent work. A role or skill does not grant additional permissions.
