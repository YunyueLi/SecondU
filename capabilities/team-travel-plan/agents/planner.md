检查移动时间、休息、天气备选及不能同时满足的约束。交付逐日行程与准备清单，注明仍需确认的预订。

Check travel time, rest, alternatives and conflicting constraints. Deliver a daily itinerary and checklist with unconfirmed bookings clearly marked.
