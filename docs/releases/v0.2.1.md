# SecondU v0.2.1

## English

SecondU v0.2.1 adds a complete Finance workspace and makes everyday navigation, setup and conversations smoother.

- **Personal finance:** seven pages for overview, accounts, holdings, market data, liabilities, transactions and planning. Explore asset structure, allocation and details, and customize 13 overview components.
- **Records and planning:** enter transactions or import CSV files, organize bills and subscriptions, set budgets and goals, compare scenarios and generate weekly reports. Bring selected financial records into a conversation draft.
- **More connections:** account integrations for Moomoo, Plaid, Teller, TrueLayer, Interactive Brokers Flex and SnapTrade, plus nine financial research services in the connector directory.
- **A workspace that fits your work:** pin Finance, Agents and Library, expand Finance and Agent navigation, manage local and SSH computers, and start tasks from home suggestions. Conversation drafts keep their own text.
- **Appearance and layout:** six solid accent colors, Spectrum and custom colors, with a subtle activity glow. Refined dialogs, account filters, message bubbles, settings and mobile layouts.
- **A richer introduction:** ten bilingual guide chapters, six interactive Finance views on the website and refreshed product images. Open the full workspace from a preview and return to the same reading position.
- **Time display fixes:** quote timestamps follow the market's time zone, and date-only NAV observations keep their original calendar date.

## 简体中文

SecondU v0.2.1 带来完整财务工作区，并改进日常导航、配置与对话体验。

- **个人财务：**总览、账户、持仓、行情、负债、收支和计划七个页面；查看资产结构、占比和明细，自定义 13 类总览组件。
- **记录与规划：**手动记录或导入 CSV，整理账单与订阅，设置预算和目标，比较情景方案，生成财务周报；选中的财务记录可带入对话草稿。
- **更多连接：**新增 Moomoo、Plaid、Teller、TrueLayer、Interactive Brokers Flex、SnapTrade 账户集成，连接器目录加入九项金融研究服务。
- **贴合自己的工作区：**固定财务、Agents 和资料库，展开财务与 Agents 分组，管理本机与 SSH 电脑，从首页建议开始任务；各段对话分别保留草稿。
- **外观与布局：**六种单色、多彩及自定义强调色，配合柔和的运行提示；改进弹窗、账户筛选、消息气泡、设置和手机布局。
- **更完整的产品介绍：**十章双语引导、官网六个可交互财务视图及更新的产品图片；从预览打开完整工作区，关闭后回到原来的阅读位置。
- **时间显示修正：**行情时间跟随市场时区，只有日期的净值记录保留原始日期。
